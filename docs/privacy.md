# Privacy Policy for Tullab

**Last updated: September 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Tullab ("the Application", "Tullab", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Application.

Tullab is designed as a privacy-first, local-first application. The core data you create in Tullab is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or synchronized with a server operated by us.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Data You Enter Into Tullab

Tullab allows you to manage information such as:

* Student names
* Lesson information
* Schedules
* Notes
* Homework and related academic information

This core application data is stored locally on your device.

**We do not operate a cloud service that stores or synchronizes this data, and we do not have access to the content you enter into Tullab through the Application.**

Your locally stored data remains under your control.

### 1.2 Analytics Data

Tullab uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the Application is used and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Analytics may collect technical and usage information such as:

* Device type and model
* Operating system and version
* Application version
* General usage and interaction information
* Diagnostic and performance information
* IP address and other information that may be processed by Google as part of its services

We configure and use analytics for application improvement and product analysis. We do not intentionally send the content of your locally stored academic data, such as student names, notes, homework, or lesson content, to Google Analytics.

### 1.3 Crash and Diagnostic Data

Tullab uses **Firebase Crashlytics** to detect, diagnose, and fix application crashes and stability issues.

Crashlytics may collect information such as:

* Device type and model
* Operating system and version
* Application version
* Crash information and stack traces
* Application state and diagnostic information
* Other technical information necessary to diagnose application problems

Crashlytics is used to improve the stability, reliability, and performance of Tullab.

We do not intentionally include the content of your locally stored academic data in crash reports.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Tullab uses the following third-party services:

* **Google Analytics for Firebase**
* **Firebase Crashlytics**

These services are provided by Google and may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Tullab does not sell your personal information.

## 3. Internet Connectivity

Tullab's core functionality is designed to work locally on your device and does not require an account, cloud synchronization, or a Tullab-operated backend.

However, the Application may use an internet connection to transmit analytics, crash, and diagnostic information through the third-party services described above.

If network connectivity is unavailable, the core locally stored functionality of Tullab remains available. Analytics and crash information may be delayed or unavailable until connectivity is restored.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Your core Tullab data is stored locally on your device. As a result, the security of this data also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

We recommend using appropriate device security measures, such as a screen lock, passcode, or biometric authentication.

Because we do not store your core application data on our servers, we cannot recover that data if your device is lost, damaged, reset, or if the Application and its local data are deleted.

## 5. Data Backup and Export

Tullab provides a data export/backup feature that allows you to export your locally stored application data.

You are responsible for keeping any exported backup files secure.

If you choose to share or store a backup file outside your device, that copy may be accessible to other people or services depending on where you store it.

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete your locally stored Tullab data using the application's data reset functionality.

Because the core application data is stored locally, deleting it from your device generally cannot be undone by us.

Deleting the Application may also remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and operating system.

Please note that deleting local application data does not necessarily delete information that has already been processed by third-party services such as Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Such information is handled according to the respective third-party service's policies.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Tullab is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through the Application, please contact us.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding personal information processed by third-party services used by Tullab.

Because Tullab does not maintain a cloud database containing your core application data, we generally cannot access, modify, or retrieve the locally stored content you create in the Application.

For information processed by Google services, you may need to exercise applicable rights directly with Google.

## 9. Links to Other Websites

Tullab may contain links to websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
